# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution. This list covers **VAPT method and tooling**: scoping and authorization, methodology standards (PTES, OSSTMM, NIST SP 800-115, OWASP), MITRE ATT&CK, the Cyber Kill Chain, threat modeling for scoping, free/open-source tools for each testing phase, building a legal practice lab, and reporting.

## What belongs here

- Official methodology standards and their supporting documents.
- Free or genuinely-free-tier tools for reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, and reporting.
- Legal, authorized practice platforms and deliberately vulnerable targets for self-hosted labs.
- Templates for scoping, rules of engagement, findings, and reports.
- Certification and training paths specific to VAPT.
- Government and standards bodies that own the frameworks referenced here.

## What does not belong here

- Any tool, script, or technique whose only practical use is unauthorized access, or that is presented without an authorized-use context.
- Working exploit code for unpatched (0-day) vulnerabilities.
- Framework-specific compliance content (covered by the companion Security Frameworks and Federal Compliance lists — see Related Lists in the README).
- Vendor marketing content without a substantive free tier, open-source component, or named feature.
- Self-promotion of products you have not used or do not work with.

## How to submit

1. Open an issue titled `[Submission]: Resource Name`, or open a PR directly.
2. Add the entry in alphabetical order within the relevant section, in the format: `- [Name](URL) - one sentence on what it is, one sentence on why a practitioner would care.`
3. If proposing a new template, include a filled-in example, not just an empty structure.
4. Explicitly state the resource's legal-use context if it is not obvious (e.g., "requires an isolated lab" or "authorized platform").
5. If you are the author or maintainer of the resource, disclose that in the issue or PR description.

## Style

- American English in the description copy.
- No emojis.
- Avoid banned vocabulary (delve, leverage, harness, robust, seamless, holistic, transformative, paradigm). Plain language wins.
- Flag any tool with a meaningful legal caveat (wireless testing, password cracking) explicitly, as the existing entries do.

## Quality bar

- Must be publicly accessible (open-source, free tier, or a legal practice platform), or a mature commercial platform with a named feature set.
- Must be actively maintained (commit within 12 months, or a standards body with an active update cycle).
- Description must be factual, with no performance or effectiveness claims that cannot be verified.

## Review

A maintainer reviews PRs within seven days. Most PRs that meet the criteria above land within two weeks.
